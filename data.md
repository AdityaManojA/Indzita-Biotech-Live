## 10. Major Milestone & Press Release: ₹7 Crore MAHA MedTech Mission Grant

### Headline
**No biopsy, just blood: RGCB researchers develop blood test for early cervical cancer detection, ₹7 crore grant to fund device**

- **Dateline**: THIRUVANANTHAPURAM, KERALA
- **Grant Award**: ₹7 Crore (~$840,000 USD)
- **Granting Initiative**: MAHA MedTech Mission launched jointly by ANRF (Anusandhan National Research Foundation) / ICMR (Indian Council of Medical Research) / Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation
- **Collaborative Consortium**:
  1. **BRIC - Rajiv Gandhi Centre for Biotechnology (BRIC-RGCB)** (Thiruvananthapuram)
  2. **IndZita Biotech Private Limited** (Thiruvananthapuram)
  3. **Karkinos Healthcare Private Limited** (Mumbai)
- **Core Investigators & Leadership**:
  - **Dr. Ani V Das** (Lead Researcher & Director, IndZita Biotech / BRIC-RGCB)
  - **Dr. Mahendran K.R** (Collaborator & Membrane Biophysics / Nanopore Specialist, BRIC-RGCB)
  - **Dr. Jackson James** (Collaborator & Neurobiology / Stem Cell Dynamics Specialist, BRIC-RGCB)
  - In strategic partnership with **IndZita Biotech** (hardware & diagnostic platform engineering) and **Karkinos Healthcare** (distributed clinical oncology trials and screening network).

### Technology Breakdown
- **Detection Biomarker**: Blood-circulating piRNA (PIWI-interacting RNA) dysregulated in early-stage cervical cancer.
- **Analytical Modality**: Advanced **Nanopore Technology** measuring single-molecule electrical resistive pulses at the nanoscale.
- **Operational Paradigm**: Fully automated, sample-to-answer point-of-care device. Requires only a simple blood draw—zero need for invasive cervical biopsies, speculums, or specialized pathology technician training.
- **Public Health Impact**:
  - Eliminates the logistical and cultural barriers of invasive Pap smears and pelvic examinations.
  - Substantially cheaper, faster (results in minutes), and decentralized for smaller towns and primary healthcare centers across India.
- **Intellectual Property & Validation**:
  - Technology is patented.
  - Cleared lab-scale validation in human cell cultures and mouse models.
  - Pilot human blood study completed at laboratory scale.
  - Early-stage automated clinical prototype fabrication currently underway.
